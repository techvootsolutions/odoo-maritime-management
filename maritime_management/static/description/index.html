Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about installation, compatibility, and maritime features

Does Maritime Management require third-party tools or DA-Desk?
No. Maritime Management runs natively inside Odoo 19 (Community and Enterprise), extending standard Maintenance, Purchase, and Accounting into an integrated maritime control layer without external software subscriptions.
Which Odoo versions are supported?
Built exclusively for Odoo 19 across Community Edition, Enterprise Edition, and Odoo.sh deployments.
How does Superintendent and Finance role toggling work?
The Control Center dashboard features dynamic role scope filtering. Users can switch between Superintendent mode (focusing on technical KPIs, VITAL jobs, and arrivals) and Finance mode (focusing on PDA approvals, FDA variance, and 3-way matching).
Can multiple Maintenance Requests be linked to a single Purchase Order?
Yes. Port agents and buyers can consolidate multiple Maintenance Requests into a single Purchase Order line-by-line while maintaining 100% end-to-end traceability back to individual maintenance jobs and vessel defects.
What is the difference between PDA and FDA in this module?
Proforma Disbursement Account (PDA) represents pre-arrival estimated port expenses submitted by the agent for budget approval before expenditure. Final Disbursement Account (FDA) tracks actual incurred costs after vessel departure for variance analysis (% savings/overrun) and 3-way matching.
How do automatic email notifications work?
Emails are automatically queued and logged in Chatter on key workflow events: port call activation (notifies agent), MR submission (notifies superintendent), PDA submission/approval (notifies finance and agent), PO confirmation (notifies team), and FDA settlement.
